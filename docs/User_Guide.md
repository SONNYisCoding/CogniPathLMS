# User Guide - CogniPath LMS

Welcome to CogniPath LMS! This guide will help you navigate the platform and make the most of your personalized AI-driven learning experience.

---

## 1. Getting Started
### Signing In
- Click the **Sign In** button on the landing page.
- Use your **Google Account** to authenticate.
- Your profile (name, photo) will be automatically synchronized.

---

## 2. Creating Your First Course
### The Generation Form
1.  **Define Your Goal:** Tell the AI exactly what you want to learn (e.g., "Master React State Management" or "Intro to Quantum Computing").
2.  **Select Your Level:** Choose between Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
3.  **Upload Context (Optional):** You can upload PDFs, Word docs, or Text files. The AI will use these documents as the primary source material for your curriculum.
4.  **Generate:** Click the button and wait a few seconds for the AI to build your personalized Learning Path.

---

## 3. Navigating the Learning Path
### The Dashboard
- Your generated courses appear as cards on your dashboard.
- Each card shows the course title, goal, and creation date.
- Click a card to enter the **Learning Workspace**.

---

## 4. The Learning Workspace
### Course Pane (Left)
- View the overall syllabus and your progress across modules.
- Click a module to expand its sub-topics.
- Click **"Start Lesson"** to generate the content for that module.

### Lesson Pane (Center)
- Read the AI-generated lesson content in a clean, distraction-free markdown viewer.
- Lessons are tailored to your goal and incorporate any documents you uploaded.

### AI Tutor Pane (Right)
- Need clarification? Ask the AI Tutor!
- The tutor has "contextual awareness"—it knows exactly which lesson you are reading and can explain complex concepts in detail using the Socratic method.

---

## 5. Advanced Features
### Regenerating Lessons
- If a lesson isn't what you expected, click the **Regenerate** button.
- Provide feedback (e.g., "Too advanced" or "Needs more examples").
- The AI will clear the previous chat history for that module and write a fresh version for you.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting
- **Generation Fails:** Ensure your goal is descriptive. If you uploaded files, check that they are not corrupted or password-protected.
- **Chat Not Responding:** Check your internet connection. The AI requires an active connection to the Gemini API.
- **Lost Data:** Ensure you are signed in with the correct Google account. All data is tied to your unique ID.
