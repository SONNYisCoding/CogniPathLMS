# CogniPath LMS User Guide (Step-by-Step)

Below is a detailed step-by-step guide to starting your personalized learning journey with the CogniPath AI Tutor system.

---

### Step 1: Getting Started & Logging In
When you first access the application, the system will ask you to enter your learning goal and current level.
However, for the AI to store and personalize a learning path specifically for you, you need to log in.
* In the **Start Your Journey** form, fill in your goal (e.g., "What is your specific mission").
* Select your level (e.g., "Beginner").
* Click the **Sign in to Start Journey** button (with the lock icon) or the **Sign In** button in the top right corner to log in with your Google account.

![Getting started screen without login](assets/guide_step1.png)

---

### Step 2: Creating a Learning Path
After successfully logging in (you will see your avatar and name - e.g., "Triet" in the top right corner), you are ready to create a learning path.
* At this point, the lock button has changed to a blue **Create Learning Path** button.
* Double-check your learning goal information.
* Click **Create Learning Path** for the Gemini system to begin analyzing and outlining a detailed curriculum tailored just for you.

![Ready to create path screen](assets/guide_step2.png)

---

### Step 3: Managing Learning Paths (Dashboard)
All the learning paths you have created will be securely stored in the cloud. To review them, click the **My Paths** tab on the navigation bar.
* Here, you will see the **My Learning Paths** page.
* Paths are displayed as visual cards, such as: *Data Science Foundation for Beginners*, *Junior English Accelerator*, etc.
* Each card provides a summary: Level (Beginner), estimated time (e.g., 12 Weeks), and the number of modules (e.g., 6 Modules).
* Click the **+ New Path** button if you want to learn a new skill.

![Learning paths management page](assets/guide_step3.png)

---

### Step 4: Exploring the Path Overview
When you click on a specific learning path card in Step 3, you will be taken to the **Path Visualizer** screen.
* **Left Panel**: Displays an overview of your entire goal and a list of Modules. For example: Module 1 *"Foundations of Academic & Workplace English"*. Each Module will detail the Topics you will learn.
* **Right Chat Window**: The AI assistant (CogniPath Tutor) will send a greeting *"Hi! I've designed this learning path for you..."*. You can start chatting with the assistant immediately to ask general questions about this entire path. The assistant has already been provided with the context of all the modules.

![Path Visualizer and Overview Chat screen](assets/guide_step4.png)

---

### Step 5: Starting a Lesson & In-depth Interaction
Click on a specific Module (e.g., Module 2: *"Mastering Professional Communication..."*), and the system will take you to the detailed learning interface.
* **Left Screen**: Will show a waiting state with the message *"Ready to start? I have prepared a detailed lesson plan..."*.
* Click the blue **Start Lesson** button. The AI Engine will then generate the entire detailed lesson content, formatted visually in Markdown.
* **Right Chat Window**: This will now switch to the specific context of this lesson (Local Context). Any question you type into the *"Type your question..."* box will be answered by the AI based exactly on the lesson content currently displayed on the left.

![Module Detail View ready to generate lesson content](assets/guide_step5.png)

---

Wishing you a wonderful learning experience with **CogniPath LMS**!
